Fallout 76 Weapon Tier List — Update 70 / September 2026
General PvE / endgame-oriented ranking.

S = exceptional/top-tier
A = excellent
B = good/viable
C = decent/situational
D = weak/niche
F = extremely weak/novelty

Assumptions:
- Max-level weapons and a reasonably optimized build.
- Normal legendary effects are assumed; a perfect god roll can move a weapon up in practical value.
- Ranking considers general PvE usefulness, DPS, VATS/crit potential, ammo economy, range, utility and ease of use.
- Named unique weapons are included when they function as meaningful separate weapon variants.
- Weapon skins that replaced former unique items are not treated as separate weapons.
- This is a practical tier list, not a claim that every weapon within a tier is identical.

Sources checked:
- Bethesda Fallout 76: The Slasher / Update 70 release notes (September 15, 2026).
- Fallout Wiki current weapon roster.
- FO76DB current Update 70 ranged and melee tier references (September 2026).
- Nukes & Dragons Fallout 76 database.
